### How to Tell If It Is a Crucial Conversation
- Is the stake high?
- Does your argument vary from that of the people sitting in font of you?
- Does emotion run high?

### A Successful Crucial Conversation Means No Fool's Choice

When having a crucial conversation, many people believe they have only two choices:
- Talk frankly and your relationship breaks down.
- Avert the controversial or significant topic to maintain the relationship.

Indeed we don't have to make a fool's choice during critical conversation.
The key is to ensure information is flowing freely when communicating.

### Focus on What You Want and What You Don't

There are 3 mistakes that we frequently make during a crucial conversation.

- Firstly, we see the conversation as a war and try to be the winner.
 We debunk the idea of the other person so as to prove we are right.
- Secondly, we try to mete out punishment to other people.
This happens often when one said something making us embarrassed.
- The last one is to try to be a peace keeper.
 That is to try to evade the conversation in order to keep safe.

To stay alway from these mistakes, one should ensure that he or she is really focusing on the topic.
Before the conversation starts, one should clarify what to expect and what to avoid during the conversation.
Then figure out how to fulfil purpose without inducing the bad things.
