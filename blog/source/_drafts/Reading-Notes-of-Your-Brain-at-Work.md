### Prioritize Prioritization
Here prioritization means to make a plan.
As the activity of prioritization is so energy consuming, we should do it when our brain is at its best condition.
While prioritizing, put details down so as to spare your brain to do other creative things such as comparison and reasoning which are of greater value.
Make your plan based on the estimated energy consumption of the activities, i.e. hard work comes in advance of easy jobs.
A good strategy is to divide your time into blocks and then do different kinds of jobs in consecutive blocks. For example, you can spend one hour on essay writing and then do some conditioning in the next hour. In this way, you are giving your brain a chance to relax and recovery.

### Why and How to Deal with Negative Emotions

Negative emotions are managed by the our brain's limbic system.
When aroused, the limbic system will gobble energy in a frightening way, hindering the functions of other parts of our brain.
Thus, in order to help our brain work smoothly, we have to handle negative emotions carefully.
Faced with bad mood, many people choose to simply suppress it.
However, this either doesn't work or make it worse.
Luckily, we have a potent way to lessen the adverse effect of negative emotions of labeling.
Labeling is defined as symbolic descriptions of negative emotions.
With the help of labeling, limbic system arousal can be reduced substantially, hence giving a decent boost to our brain.
