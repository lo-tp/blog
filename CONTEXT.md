# Lotp's Blog

A personal engineering blog published in English, with a Chinese edition planned as a
parallel language of the same site. The author writes and translates every article by
hand; tooling only makes the second language possible.

## Language

**English edition**:
The primary, canonical edition of the blog — every article exists in English first, and
the public site at `/` is English.
_Avoid_: primary language, default site

**Chinese edition**:
The Simplified-Chinese edition of the same blog, served under `/zh/`. It is a complete
site in its own right (own home page, own tag pages, own feed), not an appendage.
_Avoid_: 繁体, Traditional, zh-cn edition

**Original**:
The English article a Chinese article was translated from. The Original is the source of
record; the translation follows it.
_Avoid_: source post, master post

**Translation pair**:
An Original and its Chinese counterpart that readers can switch between one-to-one. A
pair is deliberate and declared; two articles merely having similar titles are not a pair.
_Avoid_: mirror, duplicate

**Untranslated post**:
An Original with no Chinese counterpart. This is a legal, permanent state — not every
article is expected to be bilingual.
_Avoid_: missing translation, incomplete post

**Orphan translation**:
A Chinese article with no English Original. Not expected, and treated as a mistake to fix.

**Translation drift**:
A translation whose Original has been revised since the translation was made. Drift is
expected and tolerated; pretending it does not exist is the failure mode.

**Author translation**:
A translation written by the blog author. Machine translation is not part of this
context: no article body is produced automatically.
_Avoid_: auto-translation, MT

## Translation terminology

Canonical Chinese renderings to use consistently across the Chinese edition. The author
owns these calls; anything marked _(proposed)_ is a draft awaiting the author's decision.

**编程代理** _(proposed)_:
A coding agent — an AI tool that writes and edits code on the author's behalf.
_Avoid_: 代码代理, AI 助手 (when specifically meaning a coding agent)

**测试驱动开发（TDD）**:
Test-driven development: writing the test before the code.
_Avoid_: 测试先行的开发

**间隔重复** _(proposed)_:
Spaced repetition: reviewing material at widening intervals.
_Avoid_: 分散复习

**保留原文**:
Code, commands, file paths, API and library names, product names and people's names stay
in English inside Chinese prose. First mention of a translated concept may show the
English term in parentheses.
_Avoid_: 全中文主义
